# Brush Factory / Dynamic Draw brush images

The six `BrCircle*.png` images and `LICENSE.txt` come from
[NinthDesertDude/Dynamic-Draw](https://github.com/NinthDesertDude/Dynamic-Draw),
revision `722ccb2` (v4.0 source). Copyright (c) 2017 Joshua Lamusga, MIT license.
They are embedded unchanged to resolve the corresponding original preset names.
PaintCraft's preset reader and rasterizer are separate Rust implementations.
The original license is also embedded and accessible in the brush dialog.
