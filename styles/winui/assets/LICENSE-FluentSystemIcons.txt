styles/winui/assets/icons.bin (used by the winui, macos, classic and kde styles) contains glyph
outlines from Fluent UI System Icons by Microsoft:
  https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons
  fonts/FluentSystemIcons-Regular.ttf and fonts/FluentSystemIcons-Filled.ttf,
  commit 83b26348c811f072429427ff48362238656524fe
converted with tools/ttf2bin.py; tools/icons_fluent.txt lists the glyph used for every icon.
The licence of that repository follows unchanged.
