### Added

- `formats::json` (strict bounded JSON: exact numbers, member order kept, duplicate names and lone surrogates refused, `Limits` for bytes, nesting, values, strings and members, `stringify_pretty`), `formats::jmap` (typed records and string enums: `Field`, `Json`, `record!`, `wire_enum!`), `formats::xml` (a bounded, namespace-aware XML subset: `parse`, `parse_with(Limits)`, `escape`) and `formats::encoding` (UTF-8 with and without BOM, UTF-16 LE and BE). The code was `agent_core::json` and `agent_core::jmap`, `base::xml` and `base::encoding` until now and moved unchanged, with the same limits, errors and output; those paths remain as re-exports. The crate depends on nothing but `alloc`.
- `formats::encoding::Charset`: every single-byte encoding of the WHATWG Encoding Standard (windows-874 and 1250 to 1258, ISO-8859-2 to -16 and -8-I, KOI8-R, KOI8-U, macintosh, IBM866, x-mac-cyrillic, x-user-defined), the DOS code pages of old files (IBM437, 737, 775, 850, 852, 855, 857, 858, 860 to 865, 869) and an exact ISO-8859-1, with `from_label`, `from_code_page`, `name`, `code_page`, `whatwg`, `decode` (U+FFFD for unassigned bytes), `decode_char`, `encode` (`Error::Unmappable`) and `encode_char`. `encoding::label(name) -> Label` knows all 228 labels of the standard (`Label::Replacement`), `encoding::decode_label` is the standard's decode (a byte-order mark wins over the label, malformed input becomes U+FFFD). The tables are the standard's indexes, checked against its data; its labels and index entries are under its BSD 3-Clause License (`licenses/WHATWG-LICENSE.txt`).

- `formats::xml::Reader`, the strict pull reader: well-formed XML 1.0 with namespaces as events (`Event`: Start, End, Text, CData, Comment, Pi, Eof), `Start` and `Attr` with their namespace URIs, `ReadError` with the byte offset, `skip` for the raw text of an element, the declaration as XML 1.0 defines it, a DOCTYPE refused; bounded nesting, attributes and namespace bindings, no recursion. `formats::xml::Lenient`, the forgiving reader for files from editors: `Token`s, `decode` for references, a DOCTYPE's internal entities within a budget of bytes, broken markup read as far as it goes, `truncated`. `xml::parse` builds its tree on `Reader` without recursion; there an undefined entity is `Syntax`, more than 64 attributes are `Limit`, and names may use letters beyond ASCII. `Error` has `Depth` and `Attributes` for the reader.
- The multi-byte encodings of the WHATWG Encoding Standard in formats::encoding (ROADMAP section 13, U17): MultiByte with Shift_JIS (Windows' code page 932, with the NEC and IBM extensions and the user-defined rows), EUC-JP (JIS X 0208 and 0212), ISO-2022-JP, GBK, gb18030 (GB18030-2022, all of Unicode but U+E5E5), Big5 (with HKSCS-2008) and EUC-KR (Windows' 949, every Hangul syllable); from_label, from_code_page, name, code_page, decode (malformed input becomes U+FFFD, ASCII after a broken lead byte is kept), encode (Error::Unmappable) and encode_html (character references, as browsers submit forms). Decoder: the standard's decoders for input in pieces, for every label (UTF-8, UTF-16, the single-byte sets, the multi-byte encodings, replacement): a character cut between two pieces is finished by the next, the text is the same as decoding all at once; sniffing for the byte-order mark rule, malformed. encoding::from_code_page(n) for the encodings with a Windows code page (65001, 1200 and 1201 too; None for the others, also for 50222, whose SO and SI the standard's ISO-2022-JP does not read) and Label::name. label() names the multi-byte encodings as Label::Multi(MultiByte), decode_label decodes them. The indexes are the standard's (cjk.rs and cjk.bin, 300 KB, written by tools/gen_encodings.py from Python's codecs and the entries where the standard differs: HKSCS-2008, GB18030-2022), checked pointer by pointer against encoding_rs
