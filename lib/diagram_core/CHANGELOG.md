# Changelog of jflib-diagram-core

What changed in this crate. All of JFLib's crates share one version and are released together;
the [library's changelog](https://github.com/JFLibrary/JFLib/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) has the
whole picture. The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html) as JFLib's
[VERSIONING.md](https://github.com/JFLibrary/JFLib/blob/main/docs/VERSIONING.md) describes.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The crate, with the document model of stage JD2 of the plan
  [apps/JFDiagrammer/PLAN.md](https://github.com/JFLibrary/JFLib/blob/main/apps/JFDiagrammer/PLAN.md):
  - `Document` with foreground and background pages, masters (a stencil is a document without
    pages), styles, the document sheet, theme parts and other package parts kept as bytes;
    `Page`, `Master` and `Style` with their attributes, sheets and `Connect` entries
  - `ShapeTree` of shapes, groups, foreign objects and guides with their subshapes, file IDs
    found through a map, slots (`ShapeRef`) that stay valid across undo
  - the ShapeSheet store `Sheet`, `Section`, `Row` and `Cell`: formula, cached value, unit and
    error as the file has them (`Value`, `Formula`, `CellError`), indexed and named rows, Del
    marks; `Extras` keeps what the model does not know at the place it stood
  - `Document::resolve` reads a cell through the master shape and the line, fill and text
    styles and says where its formula comes from (`Origin`); `rows` and `section_indices`
    merge inherited, overridden and deleted rows; `shape_data`, `user_cells`, `hyperlinks`,
    `layers` and `layer_members` show them
  - `Unit` knows every unit code of a cell and converts to inches, radians and days, `Implicit`
    resolves the page's drawing, text, angle and duration units
  - `Strings` and `Sym` keep cell names, formulas and texts once per document (an ordered index:
    texts from a file cannot make a lookup slow)
  - edits in a `Transaction` become one `Change` that undoes and redoes by inverse steps, sized
    by what it changes; `ui::history` takes it as one step. Formula and value stay a pair:
    `set_formula` leaves the cell stale for the formula engine, `set_constant` writes a value
    whose formula is the value, `set_result` stores the engine's result, GUARD is respected
  - for loaders (stage JD3, diagram_io): `Relationship` and `Document::rels` (every relationship
    of a package as read), `Document::colors_extras`, `faces_extras` and `styles_extras`,
    `Place::InShapes` and `Place::InConnects` (what stood inside the Shapes and Connects lists),
    `Document::strings_mut` (the table for `Value::parse`, `Formula::parse`, `ShapeKind::parse`)
  - `NO_ID`, the ID 4294967295 Visio gives the deleted subshapes of an instance: a `ShapeTree`
    takes any number of shapes with it, `by_id` does not find them, they do not use up the IDs,
    and `next_id` never hands it out

### Changed

- (JD8 rest) `Transaction::delete_layer` keeps the page's layers numbered without gaps: the layers
  after the deleted one move one down and the shapes' own LayerMember cells follow (as Visio numbers
  them); `render::Options` has the new field `print`
- (JD10) The router of `glue` leaves shapes with ObjType 4 ("do not lay out and route around", CN-17) and containers out of its obstacles
- The theme cells (ThemeIndex, ColorSchemeIndex, EffectSchemeIndex, ConnectorSchemeIndex, FontSchemeIndex, the variation and embellishment indices) and the quick style cells inherit from the styles: the line and font ones with line and text, the others with fill (`Category::of`); shapes took them only from master shapes and so never got the document's theme
- (JD11) The display list works out cells stored as "Themed" through the formula engine (THEMEVAL with the style lists, colour transforms, variants, line ends, fill patterns), so a design applied shows at once; `ThemeInfo` reads the format scheme's number from `vt:fmtSchemeEx` as Visio writes it; the text tool's box uses the style "Text Only" for its text too
- `instance::import_master`: a master whose NameU the document has already (the same name in two stencils) gets ".ID" in Name and NameU, as Visio names it
- (JD8) A field without cached text (a mark `<fld IX='n'/>`) shows its Field row's value in its format instead of the raw number; SHAPETEXT, TEXTWIDTH and TEXTHEIGHT count the text of a shape's fields (their Value cells are read as dependencies)
- (JD15, DM-04) `ops::new_shape`: a rectangle or an ellipse of the drawing tools takes the document's default line, fill and text styles (DocumentSettings DefaultLineStyle, DefaultFillStyle, DefaultTextStyle, those that exist; else "Normal") as a line of the line tool already did; the text box keeps "Text Only"
- (JD15, SS-18) Dynamic glue reads WalkPreference: an end whose bit is set (1 Begin, 2 End: visWalkPrefBegNS, visWalkPrefEndNS) docks on the top or bottom of its shape when that way is as short and has as few bends as the cheapest one
- (JD15, SL-04) `MasterTexts::matches` searches the QuickInfos in the five languages too

### Fixed

- (JD21) A page that grew to the left or down (automatic size, `fit_to_drawing`, `layout::enlarge_page`) left the ends of connectors glued to whole shapes behind (their _WALKGLUE has no value of the engine's): `pagesetup::shift` moves the stored points along
- (JD7) Formulas: a point computed for a cell (`PAR(PNT(...))` in EndY) is that cell's coordinate when another formula reads it -- `ATAN2(EndY-BeginY,EndX-BeginX)` of a glued line read x for EndY before. The oracle of the local files rose from 94.67 % to 95.28 % of the stored formula cells
- (JD11) THEMEVAL's estimates of JD6 settled against [MS-VSDX], Office's notes on ECMA-376 and a Visio 16 file with computed theme values: Visio's vt:lineStyles lists lead with the entry of matrix 0 (matrix i reads entry i; read i-1 before), the "Connector..." names choose the scheme as the "Line..." ones do, FillColor2 is the fill colour slice itself, a missing ln@cap is flat (1), colour transforms chain in floating point and round once, text takes the major font when the minor one is empty; own designs write the leading line style entry
- Sections with thousands of rows were quadratic to build, compute and draw: `Section::row` / `row_pos` find an indexed row by its index (rows stay sorted), `insert_pos` appends without a search, `Transaction::add_row` takes the next index without merging every inherited row
